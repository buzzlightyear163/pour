06 — Questions

Plain answers.

Is POUR live?

The desk is. Quotes use real Hyperliquid prices and real HyperEVM gas, and wallets are made in your browser. Pours run as dry runs until the launcher switches on. When it does, the lamp on the desk turns green and says Live.

Do you ever hold my SOL?

No. In a live pour, Relay takes your SOL and pays HYPE on HyperEVM straight to your launch wallet. POUR never has custody at any point.

What if the bridge doesn't fill?

Relay refunds on the chain you paid from. Nothing deploys unless the HYPE is in your wallet.

Who owns the coin?

You do. The wallet that deploys it is yours. Its key is made in your browser and never sent anywhere, so nobody can recover it for you. Save it before you pour.

Is this financial advice?

No. POUR is a tool, not a recommendation. Memecoins are extremely risky and you can lose everything you put in.

Is POUR affiliated with Hyperliquid, pump.fun, Relay or Phantom?

No. POUR uses public infrastructure from each of them. None of them endorse it.

Will POUR DM me?

Never. No support DMs, no airdrop links, no "wallet validation". Anyone who DMs you as POUR is a scammer.
